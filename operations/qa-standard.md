## What this doc must answer

- What are the failure modes? A missed source, a wrongly connected episode, a [suspected diagnosis presented as confirmed](../product/uncertainty.md), a [conflict silently resolved](../product/uncertainty.md), a missed [open loop](../product/open-loops.md), a boundary breach into [clinical advice](../trust/regulatory-boundary.md).
- Which are caught by a checklist, which need a second human, and which need clinical review?
- How much human review does a premium trust level actually require? — an [open question](../go-to-market/open-questions.md) with direct [cost](./cost-model.md) consequences.
- What is the remedy when an error reaches a customer? See [liability](../legal/liability.md).

## Why it matters

Human QA is one of the entries in the [moat table](../market/differentiation.md). It is also, per [AI positioning](../product/ai-positioning.md), the control that stops generated summaries manufacturing false certainty.

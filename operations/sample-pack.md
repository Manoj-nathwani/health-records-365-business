# Sample pack

**Status: not yet produced.**

## What this is

One complete, real reconstruction that can be shown to a prospective customer or adviser. Produced from the founder's own history during customer zero, then anonymised — or rebuilt as a synthetic history if anonymisation proves impractical.

## What it has to do

- Make [the deliverable spec](./deliverable-spec.md) tangible
- Visibly justify the gap between our [price](../commercial/pricing.md) and a £399 alternative — this is [gate 2 of the gameplan](../gameplan.md)
- Give advisers something concrete to forward — see [the adviser one-pager](../go-to-market/adviser-one-pager.md)
- Serve as the quality bar every subsequent delivery is measured against

## Open question

Whether a genuinely useful sample can be anonymised without destroying the thing that makes it persuasive — the specific, connected, surprising detail. A synthetic history may demonstrate the format but not the value.

# Cost model

**Status: not yet built.**

## What this doc must produce

A per-customer P&L for both products:

| Input | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Hours per stage, setup | Measured during customer zero and the first cohort — [labour question](../go-to-market/labour-economics.md) |
| Hours per month, ongoing | Measured across the first cohort's second and third months |
| Blended hourly cost | Founder time now; a realistic hire rate for the model |
| Non-labour costs | Retrieval fees, hosting, translation, tooling |
| Price | Each of the [test points](../commercial/pricing.md) |

Output: gross margin at £795 / £995 / £1,495 setup and £495 / £795 / £995 annual, plus the break-even hours at each.

## Why it blocks

It converts [gate 1 of the gameplan](../gameplan.md) from a guess into a number, and it is the precondition for hiring, for automating, and for settling [price](../commercial/pricing.md).

## The scenario worth modelling early

A service that customers value, priced at what they will pay, that still loses money per customer. Model it before selling, not after.

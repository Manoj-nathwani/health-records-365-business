# Brand and messaging

Source: [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §23.

**Brand:** Health Records 365
**Domain:** health-records-365.com

The brand should sound calm, premium, competent, trustworthy and service-oriented. Technology should feel largely invisible — see the [language guide](./language.md).

## Primary brand promise

> **Your healthcare, handled — wherever life takes you.**

And the sentence that always accompanies it:

> **We don't diagnose. We make sure the people who do have everything they need.**

## The foundation-layer promise

Still true, and still used where the record leads the conversation:

> **Your medical history, managed wherever life takes you.**

## Accountability formulations

These express the promise as **accountability**, which is [the differentiator](../market/differentiation.md). ([Competitive Research](../Inbox/health-records-365-competitive-research.md) §27.)

**Competitive contrast:**

> **Your providers manage their records. Health Records 365 manages your medical history.**

**Customer promise:**

> **There is finally someone whose job it is to keep your medical history together.**

**AI-era formulation:**

> **AI can reason over the records it has. Health Records 365 makes sure it has the right history.**

**Versus software:**

> **Apps give you somewhere to manage your records. Health Records 365 manages them for you.**

## Alternative expressions

> **Your complete medical history, organised around you.**

> **One medical history across every provider and country.**

> **Your healthcare may move. Your history stays together.**

## By audience

**UK leavers:**

> **Leaving the UK? Take your medical history with you.**

**Ongoing service:**

> **One medical history, maintained around you.**

**Shortest explanation:**

> **Health Records 365 keeps your complete medical history organised across doctors, hospitals and countries.**

**Slightly longer:**

> **We collect and organise your health records, make sense of what happened across years of care, identify important unresolved history, and keep everything useful as you move between providers and countries.**

**For UK leavers, longer:**

> **We help British people moving abroad take their medical history with them and keep it organised after they leave.**

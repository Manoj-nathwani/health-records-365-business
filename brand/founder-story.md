# Founder credibility and origin

Source: [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §36.

Health Records 365 has unusually relevant founder credibility for this problem.

## Background

Founder **Manoj Nathwani** is a British software engineer and global-health technologist with longstanding experience building digital-health systems.

Previous work includes the **Babyl Rwanda** national-scale telemedicine service, which handled approximately **4.5 million consultations for 2.8 million registered users** over its operating period.

Wider digital-health work has involved:

- NHS England
- Gates Foundation
- UNAIDS
- World Health Organization
- Ministries of Health

Broader work through Llama Technology Ltd / Telemedicine abc has focused on open-source and sovereign digital-health systems internationally.

## Why the origin matters

The business was not invented from an abstract "AI health" thesis. It emerged from firsthand experience trying to reconstruct a longitudinal personal medical history and discovering how difficult it was to determine what had been resolved, what was contradictory and what had simply disappeared between providers.

The angle worth using:

> Even someone with deep healthcare-system experience had to manually collect years of records and join information that no individual provider could see in full. Expecting ordinary patients to do that themselves is unreasonable.

## How much to say publicly

Concise — do not turn the homepage into a CV:

> **Built by people who have built healthcare systems before.**

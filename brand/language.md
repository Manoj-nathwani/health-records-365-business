# Language guide

Source: [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §24.

> The customer should buy a service outcome, not a technical architecture.

## Prefer

- medical history
- health records
- complete picture
- organised around you
- keep your history together
- wherever you receive care
- unresolved history
- prepare for your next doctor
- manage your medical history
- bring everything together
- your records remain yours
- ongoing record management
- new provider
- next specialist
- across providers and countries

## Do not lead with technical language

knowledge base · workspace · RAG · LLM · AI agent · embeddings · Markdown · vector database · context window · Git · filesystem · model routing · specific model vendors · self-hosted inference

See [AI positioning](../product/ai-positioning.md) for what to say instead.

## Avoid wellness language entirely

optimisation · biohacking · readiness · sleep scores · quantified self

This is a scope boundary, not just a tone preference — see [what we are not](../strategy/what-we-are-not.md).

## Avoid fear

Per [customer experience](../product/customer-experience.md): marketing should generally avoid fear. The service is about being organised, prepared and in control — relief, not alarm.

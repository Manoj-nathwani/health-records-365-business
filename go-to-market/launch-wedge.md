# Initial go-to-market hypothesis

Sources: [Competitive Research](../Inbox/health-records-365-competitive-research.md) §§16–18; [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §21.

## The target

Moving abroad is a strong acquisition trigger, but **not all emigrants have a valuable enough problem**. The target is narrower:

> **A financially comfortable British person with a meaningful existing medical history whose healthcare is about to cross a system boundary.**

The qualifying combination is:

**trigger + history complexity + ability to pay**

All three. A healthy 30-year-old moving to Spain has a trigger and money but no history — they are well served by a free PHR. See [negative validation](../market/target-customer.md).

## Qualifying triggers

Moving abroad · recently moved · seeing a new specialist · obtaining a second opinion · joining a new healthcare provider · starting private care · managing a parent's history · treatment overseas · **returning to Britain after years abroad**.

The full set is in [customer triggers](../market/triggers.md).

## The first launch

**Customer** — affluent British person with a meaningful medical history preparing to relocate to Dubai.

**Channel** — international tax adviser, accountant, relocation company, wealth adviser or insurance broker. See [distribution and partners](./partners-and-distribution.md).

**Offer** — **Build my medical history**: collect UK records, reconstruct the history, maintain [unresolved items](../product/open-loops.md) and prepare a current record for use abroad.

**Follow-on** — **Keep my medical history current**. See [the three commercial products](../commercial/layers.md).

## Let the channel choose the geography

Dubai is a hypothesis, not a commitment. [Sojourner's Capital](./sojourners-capital.md) is European-facing — golden visas, a European tax directory — so if that channel produces demand, **Portugal and Spain move ahead of Dubai** on [the landing-page backlog](../website/strategy.md).

The qualifying combination is destination-independent. Follow whichever geography a working channel points at.

## Why Dubai specifically

The UAE has a visible market for Medical Family Office and healthcare concierge services — see [SIP and peers](../market/competitors/sip-medical-family-office.md). That signals affluent Dubai-based customers **already understand paying for healthcare coordination**. We occupy a simpler layer:

> **We don't manage your doctors or practise medicine. We make sure your medical history is properly managed wherever those doctors happen to be.**

## Known risk

Moving abroad is [table stakes](../market/table-stakes.md) as positioning — Chronicle and RinVita both work this use case. The wedge is an acquisition channel, not a differentiator. What we sell once they arrive is [accountability](../market/differentiation.md).

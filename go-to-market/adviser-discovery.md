# Adviser discovery guide

**Status: not yet written.** Step 3 of the [validation plan](./validation-plan.md).

## What this doc must contain

**Target list** — named firms and individuals across the nine [partner types](./partners-and-distribution.md), prioritised. Currently the tracker in that doc is empty.

**Script** covering:

- What their clients already ask them about relocation admin
- Whether medical history has ever come up unprompted
- What they currently do when it does
- What would make them comfortable introducing a third party to a private client
- Which [commercial arrangement](./referral-terms.md) they would actually prefer

## The hard part

Advisers are polite. "That sounds useful" is not a signal. Test for intent with a commitment that costs something: naming a specific client, agreeing a date to make an introduction, or reviewing [the one-pager](./adviser-one-pager.md) and giving line-level feedback.

Gate 3 of [the gameplan](../gameplan.md) is three advisers introducing a real client unprompted — nothing weaker counts.

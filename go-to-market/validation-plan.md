# Validation plan

Sources: [Competitive Research](../Inbox/health-records-365-competitive-research.md) §29; [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §22.

> **Distribution is partner-led.** The work is getting to the point where a partner will carry it, then getting partners.

Delivery is manual and bespoke by design, so nothing here waits on tooling.

## Clear the bar a partner will hold you to

- [ ] **1. Produce something to show** — an anonymised [sample pack](../operations/sample-pack.md) from the [existing record](../operations/reference-implementation.md)
- [ ] **2. Clear the [legal gate](../legal/compliance.md)** — [mandate](../legal/customer-mandate.md), [terms](../legal/terms.md), [insurance](../legal/liability.md). A chartered firm will ask, and "not yet" ends the conversation
- [ ] **3. Fix the offer** — one price, one scope, one honest timeline. Lead partner conversations with the premium tier; the entry price does not pay a partner enough to care
- [ ] **4. Sell three to five directly**, at full price, from the [warm network and expat communities](./first-customers.md)
- [ ] **5. Deliver them by hand**, and get written testimonials plus permission for one anonymised case study

## Then go and get partners

- [ ] **6. Write the two pieces** — the records how-to and the founder story. See [publishing](./publishing.md)
- [ ] **7. [Sojourner's Capital](./sojourners-capital.md)** — research conversation now, panel application once steps 1–5 are done
- [ ] **8. Settle [referral terms](./referral-terms.md)**, after confirming what a regulated adviser may accept
- [ ] **9. Approach five more firms** with case studies in hand — see [adviser discovery](./adviser-discovery.md)
- [ ] **10. Keep one route that does not depend on a partner**, however small

## Running alongside

- [ ] Buy a [Chronicle](../market/competitors/chronicle-health.md) reconstruction — every competitor profile held is second-hand

## The measure

> **Paying customers with no personal obligation to buy**, then **partner-originated customers**.

The second number is the one that says this scales. Instrumentation in [the sales motion](./sales-motion.md).

## Not being tested yet

- **Labour cost.** Hand-crafting is accepted at this stage — see [the labour question](./labour-economics.md)
- **Scalable retrieval.** Formal data requests per provider, bespoke per customer
- **Software.** Built per edge case, after the edge case exists

# The sales motion

## The conversation

**Ask what happened, not what they want.**

> *"What happened last time a doctor asked about your history?"*
>
> *"Where are your records now — all of them?"*
>
> *"What did it cost you the last time something went missing?"*

Past behaviour predicts payment; stated interest does not. Someone who cannot recall an incident is not a buyer yet, whatever they say about the idea being useful. The full script is in [the customer discovery guide](./customer-discovery.md).

**Then show, do not describe.** The [sample pack](../operations/sample-pack.md) is the pitch. A real record, opened and scrolled through, does more than any explanation of what the service does.

**Lead with the office, and log what lands.** The pitch is *"we handle your healthcare wherever you are — the record, the planning, the arranging — and we never diagnose."* Watch which element the prospect grabs: if it is consistently the record alone, that is a [reversal signal for the identity](../strategy/identity.md); log it.

## What can honestly be promised

Records are obtained by formal data request to every provider, including the NHS. That has a **statutory response window that has to be confirmed and then built into the promise** — see [the retrieval playbook](../operations/retrieval-playbook.md).

The consequence for selling: **the full picture takes months, not weeks.** Say so first, unprompted.

- Under-promising the timeline costs one sale occasionally
- Over-promising costs the referral, the testimonial and the case study — which are the entire reason [the first customers](first-customers.md) exist

The same applies to completeness. The honest promise is **exhaustive pursuit, never guaranteed completeness** — evidenced in [the reference implementation](../operations/reference-implementation.md), where three documents proved permanently unobtainable at maximum effort. Say this before they sign, never after something turns out to be missing.

## Price

**State it plainly, then stop talking.**

The characteristic failure for a technically-minded seller is continuing to talk after the number, which reads as apologising for it. Say the price, say nothing, let them answer.

### Never discount. Add scope.

A discount teaches the market a lower price and corrupts the [price test](../commercial/pricing.md) the first customers exist to run. When a founding customer wants a reason to say yes, **give them more rather than charging less** — an extra year of management, a family member's record, an additional provider pursued.

The founding-customer trade is explicit and fair:

> Full price, plus more scope than later customers get, in exchange for a written testimonial and permission to use an anonymised sample.

That buys the two assets [the adviser channel](./partners-and-distribution.md) cannot open without, and it keeps the price anchor intact.

## Objections

| They say | The answer |
| --- | --- |
| *"I could do this myself"* | Yes. Here is exactly how — and it took me months, and I build health systems for a living |
| *"£995 is a lot"* | Compared to what? It is one or two private consultations abroad, and it is the thing that makes those consultations useful |
| *"Chronicle is £399"* | Different product. They reconstruct and hand it over. Nobody is responsible for it afterwards — see [the landscape](../market/competitors/landscape.md) |
| *"Can't I just use the NHS App?"* | It holds what one system knows. Your history is in five — see [the problem](../strategy/problem.md) |
| *"I'm healthy"* | Then this probably is not for you yet. Honest disqualification, and it protects the referral |

**Disqualify openly.** [Not everyone is a customer](../market/target-customer.md), and a founder weak on sales converts better by telling people when the answer is no. It is the most credible thing available to say.

## What to measure

| Metric | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Enquiries per week | The only leading indicator |
| Enquiry → conversation | Is the message landing |
| Conversation → payment | Is the offer landing |
| **Paying customers with no personal obligation** | The single number that constitutes validation |

Everything else is decoration.

# Customer discovery guide

**Status: not yet written.** Step 2 of the [validation plan](./validation-plan.md) is currently a number with no method.

## What this doc must contain

**Screener** — confirming the qualifying combination of trigger, history complexity and ability to pay, per [the launch wedge](./launch-wedge.md). Someone missing any one of the three is the wrong interview.

**Interview script** covering:

- What they currently do about their records, and what it cost them last time it mattered
- Whether they have ever been unable to answer a doctor's question about their own history
- Whether they would recognise an [unresolved thread](../product/open-loops.md) as valuable or alarming
- Willingness to **delegate** rather than self-serve — the trait most easily missed in [targeting](../market/target-customer.md)
- Reaction to price, asked after value rather than before

## Method note

Ask about past behaviour and actual spend, not about interest. Every question in [open questions](./open-questions.md) that this is meant to answer should map to something the interviewee has already done, not something they say they would do.

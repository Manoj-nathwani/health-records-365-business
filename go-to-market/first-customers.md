# The first customers

## Partners multiply proof; they do not create it

Partner distribution is the plan, and it is the right one — the [validated model](./partners-and-distribution.md), with [one warm relationship](./sojourners-capital.md) already in place.

It has one precondition. **A firm introduces a third party to a private client only when a bad outcome cannot rebound on them.** Nothing establishes that except customers who have already been through it. Offering a share of revenue does not substitute: a share of nothing is nothing, and any adviser works that out in the first minute — see [referral terms](./referral-terms.md).

So a small number of customers have to come from somewhere other than a partner. **Three to five is enough.** This is not a direct-sales programme; it is the minimum evidence that makes the partner conversation real.

## The unfair advantage

**The founder lives inside the target market.** A British expat self-funding private care across Southeast Asia is not a persona to be researched — it is the room he is already in. The qualifying combination from [the launch wedge](./launch-wedge.md) describes people he meets socially.

The usual early-stage constraint is access to the buyer. Here access is free and the only constraint is knowing what to say — see [the sales motion](./sales-motion.md).

## Where the first few come from

Ranked by speed, fastest first.

| Source | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Warm network** — personal and professional | Fastest route to customer one. The global-health background carries credibility a stranger cannot verify |
| **Someone mid-crisis abroad** — just had a scare, needs history they cannot get | Trigger live and painful; converts fastest of any cold contact |
| **Long-stay visa cohorts** — Thailand, Indonesia, UAE, Portugal, Spain | Mid-transition, admin appetite already engaged, money confirmed by the visa itself |
| **Expat communities per destination** | Where the [triggers](../market/triggers.md) get posted out loud |

**A friend who pays full price counts.** A friend given it free does not — that validates nothing and produces a testimonial nobody believes.

## The lowest-effort activity that works

**Answer the record questions asked publicly in expat communities.** *"How do I get my NHS records before I move?"* gets posted constantly, and each one is a buyer announcing themselves.

It suits this founder specifically: no cold outreach, no pitch, no persona, and it rewards knowing the answer in detail. **Answer completely and do not pitch** — an answer ending in a sales line converts worse and gets removed from most communities.

## What has to exist first

Four things, and no more:

- **Something to show** — an anonymised [sample pack](../operations/sample-pack.md). The founder can show a real, complete, working record; nobody else in this market can show their own
- **A price and an honest timeline** — see [the sales motion](./sales-motion.md)
- **[One page](../website/homepage.md)**, so the service survives being searched by someone deciding whether to hand over their medical history
- **The [legal gate](../legal/compliance.md) cleared**, since acting as an agent for another person is untested and a partner will ask about it regardless

No content programme, no funnel, no second page.

## The measure

> **Paying customers with no personal obligation to buy.**

Interest, encouragement and "that's a great idea" measure nothing.

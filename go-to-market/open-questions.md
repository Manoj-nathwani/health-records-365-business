# Open questions

Sources: [Competitive Research](../Inbox/health-records-365-competitive-research.md); [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §39.

Questions to answer by delivering, not by analysing. Where a belief already has market evidence behind it, it lives in [thesis confidence](../market/thesis-confidence.md) instead.

## Unit economics

- How many human minutes does it take to build and maintain an excellent medical history? — the biggest unknown in the business, see [labour economics](./labour-economics.md)
- How much record-retrieval work should we do directly, versus coaching the customer through it?
- How much human review is required to maintain the trust level expected of a premium service? — see [AI positioning](../product/ai-positioning.md)

## Willingness to pay

- Will customers pay £800–£1,500 setup when a £399 alternative exists? — see [the missing middle](../market/missing-middle.md)
- What recurring price feels natural? Test £495 / £795 / £995 — see [pricing](../commercial/pricing.md)
- How much do customers care about UK data hosting and portability? — see [data sovereignty](../trust/data-sovereignty.md)

## Value and outputs

- Which outputs create the strongest immediate value: timeline, current summary, open items, lab trends or provider packs? — see [outputs](../product/outputs.md)
- Does the [open-loop lifecycle](../product/open-loops.md) get used, or do customers only value the initial detection?
- How frequently do customers use conversational access to their history? — see [ask your medical history](../product/ask-your-history.md)

## The office identity

- Which element of the pitch closes — the record, the handling, or the pair? A consistent record-only pattern is [a reversal signal](../strategy/identity.md)
- How many episodes of care does a member actually generate a year — and does the £795 membership survive that number? See [labour economics](./labour-economics.md)
- Will customers let us book on their behalf, or do they want the research and prefer to book themselves?
- Does the [handover pack](../product/navigation.md) actually change how clinicians treat the customer — the "taken seriously" bar, tested in real appointments

## The recurring relationship

- Will customers form the habit of forwarding new medical records? — [the retention loop](../commercial/retention-loop.md) depends on it
- How often do internationally mobile customers generate new records worth incorporating?
- Which situational service drives the most recurring engagement: appointments, new-country setup, annual review or ongoing ingestion?

## Channel

- Which partner channel converts best?
- Do accountants and tax advisers see medical-history migration as a natural referral?
- Are relocation firms stronger partners than accountants?
- Do wealth advisers or private-health insurance brokers have better customer fit?

All four feed [distribution and partners](./partners-and-distribution.md).

## Operations and reach

- Can we build a credible expected-source list for a customer who cannot remember their own providers? — see [the stewardship system](../product/stewardship-system.md)
- Does "returning to Britain after years abroad" convert as well as leaving? — see [triggers](../market/triggers.md)
- Which countries deserve dedicated landing pages first? — see [website strategy](../website/strategy.md)

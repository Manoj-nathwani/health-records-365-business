# Distribution and partners

Source: [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §20.

The most attractive early acquisition model is likely **B2B2C partnerships** rather than relying only on consumer advertising.

The ideal partner already has a trusted relationship with someone going through a life transition such as leaving the UK.

## Candidate partner types

1. international tax advisers
2. accountants specialising in UK emigration
3. UK-to-UAE relocation firms
4. wealth managers
5. international financial advisers
6. private medical insurance brokers
7. immigration and visa advisers
8. expat concierge firms
9. international private-health providers

Their customer is already asking:

> "What do I need to sort out before I leave?"

Health Records 365 adds another answer:

> **Make sure your medical history leaves with you too.**

## Timing

**Referral asks open after [the first customers](first-customers.md).** An adviser introduces a third party to a private client only when a bad outcome cannot rebound on them, and only case studies establish that. Asking early spends relationships that are hard to recover — a "no" now is close to permanent.

**Research conversations open immediately**, and warm ones carry none of that risk. [Sojourner's Capital](./sojourners-capital.md) is the live one: a chartered UK-exit firm the founder is already a client of, which already runs a vetted panel.

## Why this channel works

Adviser-led distribution is proven in the market: relevant players already work with family offices, private client advisers, wealth managers and insurance advisers, and [SIP Medical Family Office](../market/competitors/sip-medical-family-office.md) markets specifically through private client advisers ([Competitive Research](../Inbox/health-records-365-competitive-research.md) §18). It is one of our [highest-confidence theses](../market/thesis-confidence.md).

## Partner proposition

The service must be extremely easy for a professional adviser to explain:

> "We handle the financial and residency side of your move. Health Records 365 handles the healthcare side — your records, your appointments, wherever you end up."

The partner does not need to become a health adviser. They need somewhere trusted to send the problem.

## Commercial arrangements available

- referral fee
- fixed bounty per completed customer
- wholesale partner pricing — see [pricing hypotheses](../commercial/pricing.md)
- inclusion in premium relocation packages
- co-branded or white-labelled handoff later if justified

# Publishing

**Optional.** A content programme — regular cadence, audience building, search rankings — is a full job and not the one that needs doing. Partner distribution replaces it.

What is not optional is **something a partner can point at**. That is two pieces of writing, not a machine.

## The two worth writing

**One authoritative how-to: getting your complete records out of the UK.** Genuinely complete, including the parts that are tedious.

It earns its place three ways: it is what a partner can put in front of a client without vouching for anything, it is the natural thing to offer [Sojourner's Capital](./sojourners-capital.md) for their ebook or blog, and anyone who reads to the end learns how much work it is and self-selects into doing it or paying. **Publishing exactly how to do it yourself sells the service.**

**The founder story, once, written down.** Needed for the [one-pager](./adviser-one-pager.md), a partner introduction, and every first conversation.

> Built the Babyl telemedicine service in Rwanda — 4.5 million consultations, 2.8 million registered users. Still lost a five-year-overdue colonoscopy because the NHS held an address eighteen months out of date.

The gap between those two facts is the whole argument: if someone who built national health infrastructure cannot keep their own record straight, the reader has no chance. **State the facts and stop** — the point does not need making.

## The register

Dense, specific, real dates, no filler. That voice is already proven in the founder's own work and is unusual enough to carry weight on its own.

Constraints from [the language guide](../brand/language.md), without exception:

- No technical vocabulary — no RAG, no knowledge base, no model names
- No wellness language
- **No fear.** The colonoscopy story is an administrative failure, not a near-miss. Fear converts worse and attracts the wrong reader
- Records, never medicine — [the regulatory boundary](../trust/regulatory-boundary.md)

## If anything more gets written

Put it in somebody else's audience rather than building one. [Sojourner's Capital](./sojourners-capital.md) runs a blog, webinars, guides and a relocation ebook aimed at exactly this buyer. Any expat-facing publication with an existing readership works the same way. **Pitch the piece, not the service.**

## The failure mode

Building a publishing system instead of publishing — a site generator, a pipeline, analytics. Each is more comfortable than writing and none reaches a customer. Post where publishing already works.

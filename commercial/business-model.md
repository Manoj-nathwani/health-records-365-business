# Business model thesis

Source: [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §38.

**Premium service first.** Customers pay Health Records 365 to solve the problem for them — not to rent a tool.

**Software enables the service.** Technology makes delivery scalable and gives customers useful ongoing access. See [technical decisions](../product/tech-decisions.md).

**Ongoing management creates recurring revenue.** The service remains useful as healthcare continues. See [the three commercial layers](./layers.md).

**Partnerships drive efficient acquisition.** Trusted advisers introduce the service at moments when customers already need help. See [distribution and partners](../go-to-market/partners-and-distribution.md).

**Trust compounds over time.** Once Health Records 365 has maintained someone's medical history for years, the relationship becomes increasingly valuable. See [the retention loop](./retention-loop.md).

**Portability is a strength.** The record remains exportable. The business wins by being useful enough that customers stay, not by trapping their data. See [data sovereignty](../trust/data-sovereignty.md).

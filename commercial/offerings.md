# Core offerings

Source: [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §§13, 14.

## One brand, multiple entry points

Health Records 365 should remain **one singular brand**. Do not create a collection of sub-brands for different customer types. Different situations are **entry points into the same underlying service**.

The internal toolbox:

- **COLLECT** — NHS, private, hospital, overseas, personal and genetic records
- **UNDERSTAND** — timeline, episodes, laboratory history, open items, contradictions and uncertainty
- **USE** — new doctor, specialist, move country, second opinion, treatment abroad, questions and provider packs
- **MAINTAIN** — absorb new healthcare as it happens

Five verbs summarise the business:

**organise it / move it / understand it / keep it current / prepare it for care**

Each situation below can become a [landing page](../website/strategy.md) and commercial entry point without changing the underlying company.

> **These eight are entry points, not products.** We sell [one membership architecture](./layers.md) — onboarding, membership, premium — and every offering below funnels into it. Treating them as eight products would fragment delivery and imply sub-brands.

## 1. Organise My Medical History

**Who:** anyone with years of fragmented NHS, private and personal medical information. Travel not required.

**Problem:** "I have years of medical records but no clear idea what the complete story is."

**Service:** bring everything together, reconstruct the history, preserve the originals and identify unresolved threads.

> **Turn years of scattered medical records into one clear health history.**

## 2. Moving Abroad

**Who:** British people preparing to leave the UK — initially destinations such as UAE/Dubai, Spain, Portugal, Thailand and Australia.

**Problem:** "My financial life is organised for the move, but what happens to decades of medical history?"

**Service:** collect and organise relevant UK history before departure, then continue incorporating new overseas care afterwards.

> **Leaving the UK? Take your medical history with you.**

Likely the strongest early acquisition wedge — clear event, clear deadline. See the [launch hypothesis](../go-to-market/launch-wedge.md).

## 3. Already Living Abroad

**Who:** people who already left the UK and now have historic UK information plus new overseas healthcare.

**Problem:** "My old medical history is in Britain and my current healthcare is somewhere else."

> **Bring your old and new medical history back together.**

## 4. International Health Record Management

**Who:** expatriates, nomads, international families, people splitting time between countries, people using treatment abroad, or people retaining UK specialists while living elsewhere.

**Problem:** "There may never be one health system responsible for my complete history."

> **One medical history across every provider and country.**

One of the strongest recurring-service propositions.

## 5. Ongoing Health Record Management

The recurring layer across the whole business. Customers send new consultation letters, test results, reports, diagnoses, prescriptions and investigations; Health Records 365 incorporates them into the existing history.

> **Send us what happens next. We'll keep the history together.**

## 6. Prepare for a New Doctor or Specialist

**Trigger:** joining a new GP, seeing a specialist, obtaining a second opinion, undergoing a procedure or attending a private health assessment.

Prepare the relevant subset of the customer's history rather than handing every provider the entire archive.

> **Give your next doctor the history that actually matters.**

## 7. Treatment or Healthcare Abroad

**Trigger:** the customer plans to receive healthcare in another country.

Clarify what has already happened, what remains unresolved, which records are relevant to the planned care and what outcomes need incorporating afterwards. Healthcare-provider research and navigation may sit around this service where appropriate.

> **Arrive with your history ready. Bring the outcome back with you.**

## 8. Family Medical Record Management

A likely **later** expansion — for people helping manage the history of a spouse, elderly parent or other family member, with appropriate permissions.

> **Help your family keep the full medical picture together.**

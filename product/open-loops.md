# Open and unresolved history

Sources: [Competitive Research](../Inbox/health-records-365-competitive-research.md) §11; [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §7.

The highest-value concept in the service is distinguishing between:

**something that was resolved**

and

**something that simply disappeared from the record.**

## Examples of open threads

- a consultant recommended repeat testing but no later result is found
- a referral was made but no outcome appears
- a sample failed and there is no evidence it was repeated
- a diagnosis was entered but questioned in contemporaneous notes
- the same abnormal laboratory pattern appears across several years or providers
- a treatment continues while the original rationale becomes unclear
- a follow-up was expected but never recorded
- two providers hold contradictory information
- an investigation appears to have stopped midway

## Detection is table stakes. The lifecycle is not.

Identifying an unresolved item is useful, but competitors already do it — see [table stakes](../market/table-stakes.md). The value is in maintaining the **lifecycle**:

```text
Repeat test recommended
        ↓
No later result found
        ↓
OPEN ITEM CREATED
        ↓
Provider queried / customer informed
        ↓
New appointment happens
        ↓
Result supplied
        ↓
History updated
        ↓
ITEM CLOSED
```

> The differentiation is not spotting an unresolved test once. It is keeping track of unresolved medical-history threads **until their documentary state changes**.

That requires someone accountable — see [differentiation](../market/differentiation.md) — and it is tracked alongside the [source map](./stewardship-system.md).

## The framing that keeps this safe

These are **open or unresolved record threads**, not automatically clinical problems requiring intervention.

> **Health Records 365 notices when healthcare threads are opened but never clearly closed — and keeps noticing until they are.**

The service's job is to make the state of the record clear, not to tell the customer what to do about it. Per [the regulatory boundary](../trust/regulatory-boundary.md):

- Safe: "Your consultant recommended a repeat test and no later result is visible in the records we have."
- Materially different: "You should get a CT scan."

Note that "provider queried / customer informed" in the lifecycle above must stay on the safe side of that line — we chase **documents**, not treatment.

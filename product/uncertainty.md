# Uncertainty in the record

Source: [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §8.

Trust depends on not manufacturing certainty. The record may contain seven distinct kinds of information, and the service should preserve those distinctions rather than silently "cleaning" the record into something more certain than the evidence supports.

| Category | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Clinically confirmed** | Diagnoses, procedures or findings clearly established in source records |
| **Suspected or provisional** | Things clinicians considered but did not definitively confirm |
| **Historical, uncertain validity** | Labels that remain in the record but were questioned, contradicted or never followed up |
| **Patient-reported** | Symptoms, triggers, allergies or historical context supplied by the customer |
| **Family history** | Relevant information about relatives, often scattered or incompletely documented |
| **Genetic** | Supported genetic findings or consumer genetic summaries, with clear provenance and limitations |
| **Conflicting clinical opinions** | Where clinicians disagreed, the disagreement stays visible |

## The rule

> Suspected diagnoses must not become confirmed diagnoses simply because a system summarised them.

This is both a product constraint and a [trust commitment](../trust/data-sovereignty.md), and it constrains what the [AI layer](./ai-positioning.md) is allowed to do.

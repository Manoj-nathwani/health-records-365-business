# Outputs

Source: [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §10.

The exact interface can evolve, but the service should conceptually maintain the following.

**Current health summary** — a concise view of the information most relevant today.

**Longitudinal medical history** — a readable reconstruction of significant events over time.

**Health topics and episodes of care** — related information grouped together even when it came from different providers or countries.

**Long-term laboratory results** — important results brought together across years and laboratories.

**Open and unresolved items** — things whose outcome is unclear or whose expected follow-up cannot be found. See [open and unresolved history](./open-loops.md).

**Medications** — current and historical medication information with context where available.

**Vaccinations** — a consolidated vaccination history.

**Family and genetic context** — relevant patient-supplied family history and supported genetic information.

**Original source records** — the underlying evidence remains accessible.

**Provider-specific packs** — relevant history prepared for a new GP, specialist, procedure, second opinion, treatment abroad or change of country.

## The question to answer with customers

From the [open questions](../go-to-market/open-questions.md): which of these makes customers feel the strongest immediate value — timeline, current summary, open items, lab trends or provider packs? This should drive what gets built first.

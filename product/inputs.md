# Inputs

Source: [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §9.

The service focuses on medical-history information rather than fitness or wellness telemetry.

## In scope

- complete GP records
- NHS hospital records
- private medical records
- consultant letters
- discharge summaries
- imaging reports
- pathology reports
- blood and laboratory results
- screening results
- medication history
- vaccination records
- referrals
- procedure reports
- patient-provided history
- family history
- suspected allergies or triggers
- genetics summaries
- overseas medical records
- new episodes of care as they happen

## Out of scope

Fitness tracking, step counting, sleep scores, exercise recovery, calorie tracking, workout analytics, generic quantified-self dashboards, biohacking and wellness optimisation. Full reasoning in [what we are not](../strategy/what-we-are-not.md).

## Open working questions

These are unresolved and should be answered by real delivery, not in advance — see the [validation plan](../go-to-market/validation-plan.md).

- How much record-retrieval work should Health Records 365 do directly, versus coaching the customer through it?
- How often do internationally mobile customers generate new records worth incorporating?
- Will customers naturally forward new medical records as a habit?

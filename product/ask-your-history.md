# Ask your medical history

Source: [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §12.

The customer should not need to know anything about Markdown, RAG, model vendors, file structures or technical architecture. They should be able to browse their medical history, browse health topics and episodes, view long-term results, see unresolved items, find original documents, and ask questions in normal language.

The strongest customer-facing concept:

> **Ask your medical history.**

## The questions it must answer well

> Have I ever had this test before?

> Why was I originally referred to haematology?

> What happened after this abnormal result?

> Was this diagnosis ever actually confirmed?

> Show me the history of this problem.

> What health issues are still unresolved?

> I'm seeing a specialist next week. What history matters?

> My new doctor wants my previous investigations. What should I send?

> I'm getting treatment abroad. What previous records are relevant?

These double as an evaluation set: a build is working when it answers all nine well against a real reconstructed history.

## The positioning

The proposition is not:

> "We give you an AI chatbot."

It is:

> **You can finally ask questions of your complete medical history.**

Answers must stay inside [the regulatory boundary](../trust/regulatory-boundary.md) and respect [uncertainty](./uncertainty.md) — retrieval and explanation, not clinical recommendation.

# For advisers

**You handle the financial and residency side of a client's move. Their medical history is the part nobody handles.**

---

## The gap on your checklist

Your client is working through everything that has to be sorted before they leave. Tax position, residency, property, pensions, insurance, schools.

Their medical history is on nobody's list. It stays in the UK, spread across a GP practice they are about to leave, hospitals they will not return to, and private clinics with no obligation to keep in touch.

They find out it matters at the first appointment in the new country, when a doctor asks what happened and they cannot say.

---

## What we do for them

We request their records from every provider who holds them, reconstruct what actually happened, identify what was left unfinished, and keep it current as they receive care abroad.

They end up with a medical history a doctor in any country can read in ten minutes.

---

## What this means for you

- **One more thing handled**, without you becoming a health adviser
- **A clear handover.** You introduce; we take it from there and keep you informed only as much as you want
- **No clinical exposure.** We do not give medical advice, and we say so publicly — [our limits](./what-we-do-not-do.md)

---

## What your clients should know before you introduce us

We would rather you set expectations accurately than oversell us:

- **It takes two to three months.** Providers respond on their own schedule
- **It costs £995**, with the annual service after that. Published on the site, so there are no surprises
- **It is not for everyone.** A younger client with little medical history does not need this, and we will tell them so

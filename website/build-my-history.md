# Build my medical record

**Where everything starts. We collect everything that exists about your health and turn it into a history you can use — and that we can plan from.**

From £995, once. Membership begins after it, because nobody can plan your care without knowing your history.

---

## Who this is for

You have had healthcare across more than one place — different GP practices, a hospital or two, private clinics, maybe another country — and no single provider has the whole picture.

It is worth most to people who have a genuine amount of history behind them and a reason to need it: a move abroad, a new specialist, a diagnosis being reconsidered, or the sense that things have been dropped along the way.

**It is worth least to someone in their twenties who has seen one GP and never been referred.** If that is you, we will tell you.

---

## What happens

**A conversation.** Where you have had care, as far back as you can remember. We work out what should exist.

**We request it.** From everyone on that list, and we chase the ones who go quiet.

**We reconstruct it.** Connected across providers and years, with contradictions and uncertainty preserved rather than tidied away.

**You get it.** The history, every original document, and full control of both.

## What is included

- Requests to every UK provider we identify, and chasing until they answer or refuse
- Overseas records where you can give us a route to them
- Anything you already hold — old letters, results, app exports, photographs of documents
- The full reconstruction and every component described in [what you get](./what-you-get.md)
- A conversation at the end to walk you through what it found

---

## What it costs

**£995** for most people.

More where there is unusual complexity — many providers across several countries, decades of history, records that need translating. We tell you before starting, not afterwards.

## How long

**Two to three months is normal.** We begin within days; the timeline belongs to the providers, not to us.

You will see the history taking shape as records arrive rather than waiting in silence for the whole thing.

---

## What we cannot promise

**That everything still exists.** Practices close and merge, records get destroyed on retention schedules, and some providers simply do not hold what you think they hold.

What we promise is that we will pursue every source we can identify, and tell you plainly what could not be obtained and why. **A gap you know about is worth considerably more than a gap you do not.**

A history is accurate the day it is finished. Membership is what keeps it true — and what turns it from a document into a service: threads tracked, appointments planned and arranged, handovers prepared. A separate decision, made after you have seen what you got.

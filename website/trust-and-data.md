# Your records, and how we handle them

We are asking you to give us the most sensitive information you have. Here is exactly what happens to it.

---

## Where it is held

In the United Kingdom.

---

## Who can see it

The people working on your record, and nobody else. We do not sell data, share it with insurers, or make it available for research.

Nothing goes to another person or organisation without you asking us to send it.

---

## What we keep

Every original document exactly as it arrived. Your history is built on top of them, never instead of them.

This matters more than it sounds. A summary that replaces its sources cannot be checked, and a record you cannot check is a record you have to take on faith.

---

## Getting it out

At any time, in full, in plain readable files that do not need our software to open.

Not an export request, not a support ticket, not a retention period during which we keep a copy while you wait.

**We would rather be worth staying with than difficult to leave.**

---

## Deleting it

Ask, and it goes. We will tell you what is being deleted before we do it, because deletion of a medical history is not reversible and people occasionally mean *stop billing me* instead.

---

## What we will not do

- Sell or share your data
- Replace your original documents with our summaries
- Turn something suspected into something confirmed because it makes a tidier record
- Lock your history into a format only we can read

---

## Technology

We use modern tools, including automated ones, to work through large volumes of medical documents. A person reviews what they produce before it reaches your record.

We are not going to make the technology the story. What matters is that the record is right, that every claim in it traces to a document, and that a human is accountable for it.

---

## The honest limits

**We cannot guarantee completeness.** We pursue every source we can identify and chase the ones that go quiet, but some records no longer exist, and some providers will not release them. We tell you what we could not get and why.

**We are not a clinical service.** See [what we do not do](./what-we-do-not-do.md).

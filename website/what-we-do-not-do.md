# What we do not do

Most companies bury this. We think it is one of the more useful things on the site.

---

## We do not practise medicine

We do not diagnose, give a prognosis, recommend treatment, or decide which tests you should have.

We are not doctors and this is not a medical service. It is a record service.

---

## The difference, concretely

What we will say:

> *Your consultant recommended a repeat test in March 2023, and no later result appears anywhere in the records we hold.*

What we will not say:

> *You should book that test.*

The first is a statement about your record and we can stand behind it. The second is a clinical judgement about you, and it belongs to a doctor who has examined you.

This distinction is the whole design. It is also why the service is useful — **you cannot raise something with a clinician if you do not know it is sitting unfinished in your record.**

---

## We are not a replacement for a doctor

A complete history makes your appointments better. It does not replace them.

If anything in your record concerns you, the answer is a clinician, and we will help you prepare for that conversation rather than have it for you.

---

## We do not guarantee completeness

We pursue every source we can identify. We chase the ones that go quiet. We tell you what we could not obtain and why.

But practices close, records get destroyed on retention schedules, and some providers will not release what they hold. **Anyone promising a complete medical history is promising something nobody can deliver.**

What we can promise is that the search was exhaustive and that the gaps are named rather than hidden.

---

## We do not decide what is important

We do not quietly drop a diagnosis somebody once considered because a later doctor disagreed. We do not resolve a contradiction between two consultants by picking one.

Where your record disagrees with itself, we show you the disagreement. Where something was suspected but never confirmed, it stays suspected.

A cleaner record would be easier to read and less true.

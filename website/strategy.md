# Site architecture

## What the site has to prove

A stranger is being asked to hand over the most sensitive information they hold, to a company they have never heard of, and pay four figures for it.

The site's job is not to explain the service. **It is to establish that this is a real, competent, accountable operation.** Everything else follows from that.

**What reads as credible here is not what reads as credible in consumer software.** Specificity, named accountability, published prices, stated limits and visible institutional furniture. Not hero imagery, benefit adjectives, social-proof counters or a chat widget.

> The most impressive thing available is showing the actual method. Nobody else in [the landscape](../market/competitors/landscape.md) can show how they reconstruct a history, because most of them do not have a method — they have software.

## Three readers, not one

Most sites are designed for one visitor. This one has three, with different questions.

| Reader | Arrives from | Needs to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The prospect** | Search, a community answer, a partner introduction | Is this real? What exactly do I get? What does it cost? How long? |
| **The adviser** | Considering whether to refer a client | Will this embarrass me? Are they insured, registered, compliant? What will my client experience? |
| **The unaware searcher** | Looking for something adjacent — *how do I get my NHS records* | That this problem has a name, and that it does not solve itself |

The adviser is the reader most sites in this category ignore, and the one who unlocks [distribution](../go-to-market/partners-and-distribution.md). [Sojourner's Capital](../go-to-market/sojourners-capital.md) will assess panel admission partly from this site.

## Page inventory

### Core — the spine

| Page | Must do |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | State the position, name the three products, route the three readers. See [the homepage](./homepage.md) |
| **How it works** | The method, stage by stage: what we request, from whom, what happens to contradictions, how uncertainty is preserved. **The most persuasive page on the site** |
| **What you get** | The deliverable, component by component, with a real anonymised example open to read |
| **Build my history** | The one-off reconstruction — scope, timeline, price |
| **Keep it current** | The ongoing product, and why a record decays without it |
| **Prepare it for care** | The episodic product — new doctor, specialist, second opinion, treatment abroad |
| **Pricing** | All three products. Published, with what is and is not included |

### Trust — the part competitors skip

| Page | Must do |
| --- | --- |
| **Trust & data** | Where records are held, who can see them, retention, export, deletion. Concrete, not reassuring adjectives |
| **What we do not do** | No diagnosis, no treatment advice, no replacement for a clinician; and the honest completeness limit — exhaustive pursuit, never a guarantee |
| **About** | Named founder, verifiable credentials, why this exists. The origin story does real work here |
| **For advisers** | What your client gets, how referral works, our insurance and compliance position, how to contact a human |
| **Terms · Privacy · Company details** | Registered entity, ICO registration, contact address. Their absence is what makes a site look like a landing page rather than a firm |

### Situations — entry points

Each funnels into the three products. Use the `landing-page` template in [landing pages](./landing-pages/).

| Route | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| `/moving-abroad` | "I'm leaving the UK" |
| `/moving-to-dubai` · `/moving-to-spain` · `/moving-to-portugal` · `/moving-to-thailand` | Destination-specific |
| `/already-living-abroad` | "I've already moved" |
| `/returning-to-the-uk` | "I'm coming back after years away" |
| `/organise-medical-records` | "Years of records, no idea what they say" |
| `/nhs-and-private-healthcare` | "My care is split across both" |
| `/prepare-for-a-specialist` · `/second-opinion` | Appointment-driven |
| `/treatment-abroad` | "I'm having a procedure overseas" |
| `/family-medical-records` | "I manage my parent's health" |

⚠️ **A situation page must know something specific about that situation.** A Dubai page that knows what UAE providers ask for, what the insurance requires and which records travel badly is worth having. One that is the moving-abroad page with the nouns swapped is worse than not existing — it reads as SEO filler and undermines the credibility the rest of the site is building.

**Build a situation page when there is something specific to say about it**, not to fill the nav.

### Depth — the proof of expertise

| Piece | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **The complete guide to getting your UK records** | Ranks, gets shared, and is the natural thing to offer a [partner's audience](../go-to-market/sojourners-capital.md). Publishing how to do it yourself sells the service |
| **Why records go missing** | The [failure modes](../strategy/problem.md), concretely — stale addresses, dropped referrals, results nobody chased |
| **What the NHS record does not contain** | Private care, overseas care, anything pre-digitisation |

## Build order

Credibility first, reach second.

1. **Home, How it works, What you get, Pricing, About, Trust & data, What we do not do, Terms/Privacy/Company** — this set is what makes it look like a firm
2. **For advisers** — before the [Sojourner's panel application](../go-to-market/sojourners-capital.md)
3. **Three service pages**
4. **The records guide**
5. **Situation pages**, one at a time, each when there is something specific to say

## What would make it worse

- **Thin pages to fill the nav.** Fifteen shallow pages read as less serious than seven deep ones
- **Claims not yet earned.** No "trusted by", no invented testimonials, no client counts. With zero customers, say nothing about customers
- **Selling on [table stakes](../market/table-stakes.md)** — AI chat, timelines, portability. Everyone has these; leading with them positions us as software
- **Fear.** Per [the language guide](../brand/language.md), the register is calm and organised, never alarming
- **Building the site instead of selling.** It is the most comfortable work available — see [the gameplan](../gameplan.md)

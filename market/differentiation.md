# Differentiation

Sources: [Competitive Research](../Inbox/health-records-365-competitive-research.md) §§21, 24; [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §31.

## The core difference: accountability

> **Here is software for managing your medical history.**

versus

> **We manage your medical history.**

The second implies **responsibility**. Someone is asking:

- Have all important sources been obtained?
- Did the hospital respond?
- Is the pathology report missing?
- Was the specialist appointment completed?
- Is this medication entry stale?
- Was the overseas discharge summary added?
- Does the new consultant need the whole archive or a focused pack?
- Has an open referral finally received an outcome?

**That is the service.** Not the timeline, not the chat, not the export.

## What compounds

| Advantage | Why it compounds |
| --- | --- |
| Accountable stewardship | Customer delegates the job rather than operating software |
| Active completeness | Know which sources exist and pursue missing material |
| Longitudinal reconciliation | Connect events across providers and years |
| Persistent unresolved-item lifecycle | Track open history until its documentary status changes |
| Human QA | Preserve conflicts and uncertainty |
| Cross-system independence | NHS, private and overseas systems all become sources |
| Pre/post-care loop | Prepare before care and integrate outcomes afterwards |
| Record-retrieval expertise | Reusable operational IP |
| Distribution | Trusted advisers repeatedly introduce high-fit customers |
| Long customer relationship | A decade of maintained history becomes increasingly useful |
| Trust and reputation | Essential for sensitive personal information |

This is an **operations + relationship + trust + distribution moat**.

> Software makes it scalable. Software itself is not the moat.

## What is not differentiation

A living record, ongoing management, multi-provider orientation, provenance, data sovereignty, cross-border portability and AI Q&A are all [table stakes](./table-stakes.md). [VITALL](./competitors/vitall.md) sells managed ongoing records; [RinVita](./competitors/rinvita.md) sells cross-border portable history; privacy messaging is universal across the category.

Two capabilities do carry weight, stated precisely:

- **Open loops** — as a maintained [lifecycle](../product/open-loops.md), not as one-time detection
- **Founder experience** — see [founder story](../brand/founder-story.md)

# Thesis confidence

Source: [Competitive Research](../Inbox/health-records-365-competitive-research.md) §28.

How much weight each core belief carries, as of 20 August 2026. Confidence here comes from **market evidence** — competitors succeeding at something is evidence it works; it is not evidence we can do it better.

## High confidence — build on these

| Belief | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Medical records are fragmented across providers | Universal across every layer of the [landscape](./competitors/landscape.md) |
| Affluent global citizens value healthcare continuity | [Medical Family Offices](./competitors/sip-medical-family-office.md) sustain five-figure relationships on it |
| A human-managed service is valuable | [VITALL](./competitors/vitall.md) staffs Health Information Specialists; SIP uses physician review |
| Adviser-led distribution works | SIP markets through private client advisers |
| International continuity has real demand | [RinVita](./competitors/rinvita.md), SIP and others compete for it |
| Service beats SaaS for this job | The whole managed-records layer exists |

## Moderate confidence — credible, unproven for us

| Belief | Status |
| --- | --- |
| People pay hundreds for record reconstruction | [Chronicle](./competitors/chronicle-health.md) at ~£399 and VITALL at ~$499 both do it; nobody has proven our price point |
| People pay recurring fees for managed records | VITALL at ~$588/year; unknown whether our heavier service commands more |
| £795–£1,495 setup is achievable | Untested against a £399 alternative — see [the missing middle](./missing-middle.md) |
| Persistent open-loop *management* matters to customers | Logical, but nobody sells it, so nobody has demand data |

## Low confidence — the things that could sink this

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Can the service be delivered profitably? | The moat and the cost driver are the same activity — see [labour economics](../go-to-market/labour-economics.md) |
| Will customers form the habit of forwarding new records? | Product 2 revenue depends on it — see [the retention loop](../commercial/retention-loop.md) |
| Is the annual price right? | Possibly too low; possibly above what the market bears |

## Not differentiation, whatever our confidence in them

Moving abroad, AI Q&A, timelines, lab trends, provenance, data ownership and one-time open-loop detection are all valuable and all [table stakes](./table-stakes.md). Confidence that customers want them is high; confidence that they win us business is zero.

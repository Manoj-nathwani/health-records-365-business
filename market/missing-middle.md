# The missing middle

Source: [Competitive Research](../Inbox/health-records-365-competitive-research.md) §§5, 7.

The central commercial hypothesis coming out of the competitive research: there is substantial space between a one-off reconstruction service and an expensive medical concierge relationship.

## Indicative price signals

| Category | Price signal |
| --- | ---: |
| Basic DIY PHR | Free to ~$100/year |
| KeepMD-style product | ~$10/month |
| International PHR software ([RinVita](./competitors/rinvita.md)) | ~£39/month |
| [Chronicle](./competitors/chronicle-health.md) reconstruction | ~£399 one-off |
| [VITALL](./competitors/vitall.md) history acquisition | ~$499 one-off |
| [VITALL](./competitors/vitall.md) managed record | ~$588/year |
| **Potential Health Records 365** | **~£800–£1,500 setup + ~£500–£1,000/year** |
| Premium managed medical records | Thousands/year |
| [Medical Family Office](./competitors/sip-medical-family-office.md) | Many thousands to tens of thousands/year |

The bundles are not directly comparable, but the structure suggests credible space between one-off reports and full concierge healthcare.

## Evidence from the demand side

Every row above is a **supply-side** signal — what somebody charges. One demand-side figure is worth more than all of them: what this customer already pays for an adjacent service in the same decision.

**A UK-exit tax engagement at a mid-market chartered firm runs around £1,000–£1,500** *(owner, stated 2026-08-20, from two engagements at [Sojourner's Capital](../go-to-market/sojourners-capital.md))*.

So the target customer has already demonstrated willingness to pay four figures to have one strand of their departure handled properly, by someone else, once. That is the behaviour the missing middle depends on, observed rather than inferred — and it lands squarely on the £795–£995 [entry price](../commercial/pricing.md).

## Why the gap plausibly exists

[SIP](./competitors/sip-medical-family-office.md) prices standalone medical-record management in the thousands of euros per year. Chronicle prices reconstruction at £399. Nothing well-known sits between them offering *accountable, ongoing, cross-border history management* at a price an affluent-but-not-ultra-wealthy customer would pay.

## What would falsify this

- Customers at £800–£1,500 setup consistently choosing the £399 alternative
- VITALL-style pricing proving to be the ceiling rather than the floor for managed records
- The premium tier only converting when bundled with clinical services we [deliberately do not offer](../trust/regulatory-boundary.md)

The [validation plan](../go-to-market/validation-plan.md) tests exactly this.

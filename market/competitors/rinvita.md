# RinVita

Source: [Competitive Research](../../Inbox/health-records-365-competitive-research.md) §3.

**Research snapshot: 20 August 2026. Pricing and features may change.**

## What they do

An important UK competitor because its positioning overlaps heavily with the international-health narrative we intended to own. Its proposition includes:

- portable medical history
- records from multiple countries
- family profiles
- timelines
- laboratory trends
- doctor-ready summaries
- multilingual support / translation
- record sharing
- expat and relocation use cases
- second opinions
- international and concierge healthcare

## Pricing observed

| Item | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Individual | ~£39/month |
| Family plans | Higher |

PRIVAWELL and Across Medical pursue similar ideas around portable records for internationally mobile individuals and families.

## Where they are weak

RinVita is **software**. The customer still operates it. Nobody is accountable for whether the record is complete.

## What this means for us

RinVita occupies most of the ground a cross-border positioning would claim. None of the following is unique to us:

- moving abroad
- medical passports
- translation
- one health record across countries
- family health profiles
- doctor-ready PDFs
- longitudinal laboratory views
- record sharing

These remain valuable capabilities. They are not the moat — see [table stakes](../table-stakes.md).

The contrast that holds:

> **Apps give you somewhere to manage your records. Health Records 365 manages them for you.**

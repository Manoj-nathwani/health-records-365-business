# Chronicle Health

Source: [Master Business Blueprint](../../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §30.

**Research snapshot: 20 August 2026. Pricing and features may change.**

## What they do

Chronicle Health is the closest current UK competitor and is substantially more capable than a simple NHS export service. Its service includes longitudinal reconstruction, source citations, open items, contradictions and gaps, private laboratory information, genetics summaries, moving-abroad positioning and treatment-abroad use cases.

## Outputs — the Chronicle Pack

- Health Story
- Current Care
- Record Summary
- Clinical Timeline
- Topic References
- Bigger Picture
- About This Pack
- original source records
- `ai_context.md`

The `ai_context.md` output is intended to provide portable context usable with external systems such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

## Inputs they accept

NHS/private GP records, private consultations, hospital documents, diagnostics, executive health assessments, laboratory results, dental/optometry information, family history and genetics summaries.

## Commercial model

At the time of research, primarily a **one-off reconstruction and delivery service** rather than an ongoing hosted account or managed living record. It delivered an encrypted archive and stated that its retained processing copy would be deleted within a limited period. Future updates were a separate engagement or could be managed by the customer themselves.

Pricing appeared to be evolving — a dated market snapshot, not a permanent benchmark.

## Regulatory posture

Conservative: does not present itself as providing diagnosis, prognosis, treatment recommendations or medical advice. Comparable to our own [regulatory boundary](../../trust/regulatory-boundary.md).

## Where they are weak

- One-off delivery rather than a maintained living record — the gap our [retention loop](../../commercial/retention-loop.md) targets.
- Hospital/specialist retrieval capability not yet fully launched at the time of research.
- Organises overseas records the customer already possesses, but does not position itself as retrieving records directly from overseas providers.

## What this means for us

Chronicle also had a dedicated **Moving Abroad** page — meaning the UK-emigration use case is **validated but not unique**. Our [launch wedge](../../go-to-market/launch-wedge.md) cannot rely on being first to that idea.

The pricing gap is the sharpest strategic problem: our [pricing hypotheses](../../commercial/pricing.md) start at roughly 2–5× Chronicle's observed price. That premium has to be earned by retrieval work, ongoing management and service quality — not asserted.

# Competitive landscape

Sources: [Competitive Research](../../Inbox/health-records-365-competitive-research.md) §§1–6, 9, 12 (primary); [Master Business Blueprint](../../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §30.

**Research snapshot: 20 August 2026.** Treat all pricing and feature claims as dated.

> The landscape is much broader than [Chronicle Health](./chronicle-health.md). This does **not** weaken the thesis — in several areas it strongly validates it.

## Market structure — seven layers

| Layer | Examples | Core customer job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Provider-owned records | NHS App, hospital portals, future NHS Single Patient Record | Show me what this healthcare system knows |
| DIY personal health records | Guava, KeepMD, RinVita, exora, Andaman7, PRIVAWELL, Across Medical | Give me software to organise my records |
| AI over health records | ChatGPT Health, Claude + HealthEx, Tempus olivia | Help me understand the records available to you |
| One-off reconstruction | Chronicle Health | Get my historical records and reconstruct them |
| Managed records | VITALL and similar | Obtain and keep my records organised for me |
| Health advocacy / navigation | Solace and patient advocates | Help coordinate records, providers, follow-ups and care |
| Medical Family Office | SIP, Molliora, Delegata, Aperture, My-Health UAE, MARZ Medical, PinnacleCare | Take responsibility for major parts of my healthcare life |
| **Health Records 365** | **—** | **Take responsibility for my healthcare — record, plan and access — everything except the medicine** |

The opportunity is [the independent medical office](../../strategy/identity.md) between DIY software / one-off reconstruction and expensive healthcare concierge services — record-grounded, cross-border, commission-free.

## What the landscape invalidates

A long list of things we might have called differentiation are now **table stakes** — see [table stakes](../table-stakes.md). Most importantly: *moving abroad*, *AI Q&A*, *timelines and lab trends*, *provenance*, *data ownership*, *detecting a single open loop*, and *ongoing updates by themselves*.

"Chronicle reconstructs; we continue managing" is **a difference but not a defensible moat**. Chronicle has indicated potential hospital retrieval and periodic updates, and VITALL already sells a managed membership.

## The real distinction: accountability

> **Here is software for managing your medical history.**

versus

> **We manage your medical history.**

The second implies responsibility. See [differentiation](../differentiation.md).

## Interoperability is improving — do not bet against it

The NHS is developing the **Single Patient Record**; Europe is progressing the **European Health Data Space**; the **International Patient Summary (IPS)** standard exists.

Do not build the company on *"healthcare systems will always be incapable of sharing records."* Instead:

> **No individual healthcare system owns someone's complete lifelong history across every provider, insurer and country.**

Improved interoperability should **reduce collection costs** — every provider system becomes a source. See [interoperability](../../strategy/interoperability.md).

## AI makes the stewardship thesis stronger

An AI model can reason only over information available to it. It cannot reason over a hospital record never obtained, pathology stuck in another system, a Bangkok discharge note never supplied, an MRI whose provider has been forgotten, old private letters sitting in email, or failed investigations whose follow-up exists elsewhere.

That moves the hard problem upstream:

> **AI can reason over the records it has. Health Records 365 makes sure it has the right history.**

# SIP Medical Family Office

Source: [Competitive Research](../../Inbox/health-records-365-competitive-research.md) §§5–6.

**Research snapshot: 20 August 2026. Pricing and features may change.**

## What they do

Serves global citizens, expats, internationally mobile families and customers receiving care in several healthcare systems. Medical-record management includes:

- obtaining missing records
- collecting information from providers internationally
- organising records
- translation
- maintaining medical history
- physician review
- concise medical summaries
- supporting future medical care
- insurance / healthcare-navigation support

## Pricing observed

Published material has placed **standalone medical-record management in the thousands of euros per year**, with broader Medical Family Office offerings costing significantly more.

## Distribution

SIP markets through **private client advisers** — direct validation of our own [adviser-led distribution plan](../../go-to-market/partners-and-distribution.md).

## What this means for us

SIP validates that:

- internationally mobile affluent customers care about this problem
- people will pay significantly more than consumer-software prices
- adviser-led distribution is real
- translation and international-provider coordination have genuine demand

And it defines the top of [the missing middle](../missing-middle.md): there is substantial space between a £399 one-off reconstruction and a £10,000–£50,000+ Medical Family Office relationship.

## The peer group

Similar firms: Molliora, Delegata, Aperture, My-Health UAE, MARZ Medical, PinnacleCare. They are not direct competitors to a lower-priced managed-record service. They validate something more fundamental:

> **Wealthy people already pay another person or organisation to own healthcare continuity.**

Health Records 365 can take one valuable function from that world — the durable personal medical-history layer — and offer it at a much lower price.

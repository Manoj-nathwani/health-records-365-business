# VITALL

Source: [Competitive Research](../../Inbox/health-records-365-competitive-research.md) §4.

**Research snapshot: 20 August 2026. Pricing and features may change.**

> One of the most important comparables in the entire landscape.

## What they do

A managed-service model combining:

- complete relevant medical-history acquisition
- **Health Information Specialists** (humans)
- provider-facing record requests
- ongoing record acquisition
- organisation and maintenance
- downloadable/shareable records

## Pricing observed

| Item | Price |
| --- | --- |
| One record from one provider | ~$79 |
| History from one provider | ~$179 |
| More complete relevant history | ~$499 |
| Ongoing membership | ~$49/month or $588/year |

This is strikingly close to our own **build my record → manage my record** architecture. See [the three commercial layers](../../commercial/layers.md).

## What this means for us

VITALL makes the fundamental business model **more credible**, validating that:

- one-off reconstruction can cost hundreds
- ongoing medical-record management can be recurring revenue
- roughly £30–£50/month is commercially credible
- human involvement is compatible with a software-enabled service

The caution: they are already executing the model we described as our differentiator. "Ongoing management" is not a novel idea — see [differentiation](../differentiation.md).

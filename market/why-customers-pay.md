# Why customers pay

Source: [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §18.

**Time** — they do not want to manually read and organise decades of records.

**Expertise** — most people do not understand the structure of medical records, coding, episodes of care or how to reconcile contradictory information.

**Continuity** — they want their history to survive changes in doctor, provider and country.

**Confidence** — they want to know that important information has not quietly disappeared between organisations.

**Convenience** — when a doctor asks for something, they can find or prepare it quickly.

**Preparation** — the right information can be assembled before a specialist, second opinion or overseas treatment.

**Ongoing maintenance** — they do not want today's clean archive to become tomorrow's mess.

> The customer is not paying to store PDFs. They are paying for someone to keep the story coherent.

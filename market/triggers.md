# Customer triggers

Source: [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §17.

The most useful marketing and landing-page opportunities are based on **moments of need** rather than demographics.

| Trigger | Likely entry point |
| --- | --- |
| "I'm leaving the UK." | [Moving abroad](../commercial/offerings.md) |
| "I've already moved abroad." | Already living abroad |
| "I'm joining a new doctor." | Prepare for a new doctor |
| "I'm seeing a new specialist." | Prepare for a specialist |
| "I want a second opinion." | Second opinion |
| "I'm getting treatment overseas." | Treatment abroad |
| "I have years of records and no idea what they say." | Organise my medical history |
| "I use NHS and private healthcare." | Organise my medical history |
| "My care spans several countries." | International record management |
| "I'm helping manage my parent's medical history." | Family record management |
| "I want to know what investigations or follow-ups were never resolved." | [Open items review](../product/open-loops.md) |
| "I'm returning to Britain after years abroad." | Organise my medical history |
| "I'm starting private care." | Organise my medical history |

The last two come from the [competitive research](../Inbox/health-records-365-competitive-research.md) §16 and were absent from the blueprint. "Returning to Britain" is the mirror image of the emigration wedge and may be underserved.

The strongest early acquisition trigger remains:

> **I'm leaving the UK; what medical history do I take?**

Each trigger can become a [landing page](../website/strategy.md) and commercial entry point without changing the underlying company — one brand, many entry points.

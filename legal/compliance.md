# Compliance foundation

**Status: not yet established.** This gates the first paying customer.

## What this doc must settle

- ICO registration and data-controller status
- The UK GDPR lawful basis for processing **special-category health data**, and the accompanying condition
- Whether a DPIA is required (almost certainly yes, given scale and sensitivity) and completing it
- Data-processor agreements with every downstream vendor, including any AI provider — see [AI positioning](../product/ai-positioning.md)
- Retention policy: how long we hold source documents, what happens on cancellation
- Hosting and data location, matching the commitments in [data sovereignty](../trust/data-sovereignty.md)
- International transfer position for overseas records and overseas-resident customers

## How to settle it

**With specialist UK data-protection advice, not by reasoning from first principles.**

⚠️ **This is the first task on [the gameplan](../gameplan.md), not a pre-launch formality.** It gates delivery, because acting as an agent needs a [mandate](./customer-mandate.md); it gates the [partner channel](../go-to-market/sojourners-capital.md), because a chartered firm will diligence it; and it is the only item with **external lead time** — advisers and insurers work to their own queues. Start it before anything else.

Nothing in this knowledge base should be treated as a legal conclusion until that advice is obtained.

# Terms and privacy policy

**Status: not yet drafted.**

## What the terms must cover

- What is delivered and on what timescale — anchored to [the deliverable spec](../operations/deliverable-spec.md)
- What is explicitly **not** delivered: no diagnosis, prognosis, treatment recommendation or medical advice, per [the regulatory boundary](../trust/regulatory-boundary.md)
- The completeness caveat: we pursue every source we can identify, and cannot guarantee a record we were never able to obtain exists or does not
- Limitation of liability — see [liability](./liability.md)
- Cancellation, refund and what happens to the customer's records afterwards
- Renewal terms for [ongoing management](../commercial/layers.md)

## What the privacy notice must cover

The [compliance foundation](./compliance.md) settled: lawful basis, retention, processors, transfers, and the customer's rights including export — which we already commit to in [data sovereignty](../trust/data-sovereignty.md).

## Note

The completeness caveat is commercially delicate. We sell [active completeness](../market/differentiation.md) while contractually disclaiming guaranteed completeness. The wording has to hold both without hollowing out the proposition.

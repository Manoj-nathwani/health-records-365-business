# Strategic principles

Source: [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §40.

These are standing decisions. Changing one should be a deliberate act, not a drift.

**One brand.** Health Records 365. See [one brand, many entry points](../commercial/offerings.md).

**One job.** Handle the person's healthcare — the record, the plan, the access. Never the medicine.

**Independence.** No commission, referral fee or kickback from any provider we recommend or book. The moment a recommendation earns us money from the provider, it stops being information.

**Planned care only.** No emergencies, no 24/7 line, no evacuation — that is a different industry with different economics, and we say so wherever the service is described.

**Many entry points.** Sell to the customer's current situation. See [customer triggers](../market/triggers.md).

**Service language.** Explain outcomes, not architecture. See the [language guide](../brand/language.md).

**Ongoing rather than transactional.** The initial reconstruction should be the beginning of the relationship, not necessarily the end.

**Patient-centred continuity.** Do not assume any provider or country will ever hold the complete record.

**Open loops are first-class.** A record should distinguish "resolved" from "no outcome visible." See [open and unresolved history](../product/open-loops.md).

**Preserve uncertainty.** Trust is more valuable than confident-sounding automation. See [uncertainty in the record](../product/uncertainty.md).

**Preserve provenance.** Important information should remain connected to source evidence.

**Data ownership.** Portability should be a strength, not a threat. See [data sovereignty](../trust/data-sovereignty.md).

**No fitness distraction.** Stay focused on medical history and care continuity.

**AI is infrastructure.** Do not make model vendors the brand story. See [AI positioning](../product/ai-positioning.md).

**Conservative clinical boundary.** Help customers understand and use their record without casually turning the product into an automated medical adviser. See [the regulatory boundary](../trust/regulatory-boundary.md).

**Manual before overbuilding.** Prove the service with real customers, then automate repeated work. See the [validation plan](../go-to-market/validation-plan.md).

**The moat is accountability.** Continuity alone is not defensible — [VITALL](../market/competitors/vitall.md) already sells ongoing managed records. What compounds is taking **responsibility** for completeness: operations, relationship, trust and distribution. See [differentiation](../market/differentiation.md) and [table stakes](../market/table-stakes.md).

**Do not bet against interoperability.** Improving record-sharing between health systems is a tailwind that lowers our collection costs, not an existential threat. See [interoperability](./interoperability.md).

# What Health Records 365 is not

Source: [Master Business Blueprint](../Inbox/health-records-365-business-blueprint.md) §§9, 32.

Health Records 365 is not:

- an NHS export service
- a folder for medical PDFs
- an EHR for hospitals
- a fitness platform
- a wellness tracker
- a symptom checker
- a generic ChatGPT wrapper
- a replacement for clinicians
- a traditional telemedicine provider
- a one-country patient portal
- a self-service note-taking app
- an emergency or medical-assistance service
- **a Medical Family Office** — we take that world's architecture (one accountable office for a person's healthcare) but not its scope: no staff physicians, no five-figure retainer, and never the medicine itself

The goal is not to make customers administer their own medical database — nor to become their doctor.

## Not competing on telemetry

The service focuses on medical-history information rather than fitness or wellness telemetry. Explicitly out of scope:

- fitness tracking
- step counting
- sleep scores
- exercise recovery
- calorie tracking
- workout analytics
- generic quantified-self dashboards
- biohacking
- wellness optimisation

Those are different products solving different customer jobs. The corresponding language ban is in the [language guide](../brand/language.md).

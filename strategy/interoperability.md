# Interoperability and the NHS Single Patient Record

Source: [Competitive Research](../Inbox/health-records-365-competitive-research.md) §12.

Healthcare-system interoperability is improving:

- the NHS is developing the **Single Patient Record**
- Europe is progressing the **European Health Data Space**
- the **International Patient Summary (IPS)** standard exists

## The bet we must not make

Do **not** build the company on:

> healthcare systems will always be incapable of sharing records.

That is a bet against a well-funded, politically supported direction of travel.

## The bet that survives

> **No individual healthcare system owns someone's complete lifelong history across every provider, insurer and country.**

A UK Single Patient Record does not contain a Bangkok discharge summary, a Dubai consultation, a private laboratory result from 2019, or a consultant letter the patient kept in email. It also does not maintain [unresolved threads](../product/open-loops.md) or take responsibility for [completeness](../product/stewardship-system.md) across systems it does not control.

## Why this is a tailwind

Improved interoperability should **reduce collection costs**. Every provider system that becomes machine-readable becomes a cheaper source for us. The expensive part of [the stewardship system](../product/stewardship-system.md) is retrieval and chasing — interoperability attacks exactly that cost.

Health Records 365 remains the patient-centred continuity layer **above** these systems.

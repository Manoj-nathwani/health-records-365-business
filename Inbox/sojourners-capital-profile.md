# Sojourner's Capital — firm profile

**Captured 20 August 2026** from `https://thesojournerscapital.com/llms.txt`. The main site is JavaScript-rendered and returns only its title on fetch; the `llms.txt` summary is the usable capture.

Site title: **"The Sojourners Capital | UK Exit Tax Strategy"**. Search indexing additionally describes them as **CIOT and ICAEW chartered advisers** who plan and project-manage UK exits **"through a vetted panel"**, positioned **"without Big 4 fees"**.

## Clients

Their own copy says:

> "entrepreneurs, business owners, and high-net-worth families"

**Observed engagement prices tell a different story: £1,000 and £1,500 for two family members' UK-departure work** *(owner, stated 2026-08-20)*, with average client spend estimated **under £5,000** on the same basis.

That is mid-market professional, not high-net-worth — consistent with their "without Big 4 fees" positioning and inconsistent with the golden-visa and citizenship-by-investment framing. **Read the marketing as aspirational and the prices as real.**
